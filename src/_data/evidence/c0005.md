OpenMRS's own site and its listing as a UNDP Digital Public Good both cite deployment figures in the range of **70-plus countries**, **roughly 8,000 health facilities**, and implementer reports of **more than 15.6 million patient records** managed across 6,500-plus facilities in 40-plus countries. These numbers are widely repeated across OpenMRS community materials and secondary write-ups.

**These figures are self-reported.** They come from OpenMRS.org and a Digital Public Goods catalog listing that is itself substantially sourced from the project's own submissions, not from an independent census or third-party audit of live installations. They should be treated as a credible order of magnitude — OpenMRS is unambiguously one of the most widely deployed open-source clinical platforms in the world — rather than as an exact, externally verified count. A funding or procurement decision should not rest on the precision of the specific numbers.

## Sources

1. OpenMRS, [homepage](https://openmrs.org/). States the headline "70+ countries" / facility-count figures.
2. UNDP Digital Public Goods, [“OpenMRS” catalog entry](https://digitalx.undp.org/catalogs/openmrs.html). Independent-platform listing that repeats the project's self-reported scale figures, including the 15.6-million-patient-record figure.
