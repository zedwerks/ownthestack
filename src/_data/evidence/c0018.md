Moscow's **EMIAS** system is frequently described, including in openEHR-adjacent industry writing, as the largest openEHR deployment currently in operation, with figures cited in the range of **12 million-plus citizens** and **670-plus participating providers**. The source available for these figures at the time of writing was a secondary industry write-up, not a primary release from the City of Moscow or the EMIAS program itself.

**This citation is flagged, not confirmed**, for the same reason as the Slovenia citation above: the underlying claim (EMIAS is a large, real, openEHR-influenced deployment) is broadly consistent with other reporting on the system, but the specific population and provider counts have not been independently verified against a primary Russian-language or Moscow city government source. Use directionally, not as a hard number, until verified.
