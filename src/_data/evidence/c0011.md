Thoughtworks began building Bahmni in January 2013. In October 2017, Thoughtworks transferred project ownership to the **Bahmni Coalition** — a group of companies and nonprofits operating under the fiscal sponsorship of **OpenMRS Inc.** — while continuing as a major code contributor rather than the project's sole owner. This mirrors OpenMRS's own governance model: a nonprofit umbrella rather than a single commercial vendor holding the roadmap.
