Possible Health's **Bayalpata Hospital** in Nepal runs its inpatient department, operating theatre, emergency room, and outpatient department on Bahmni — a documented example of the platform handling real acute hospital workflows, not just ambulatory records. Separately, **Médecins Sans Frontières**, working with Partners In Health and Interactive Research and Development, selected Bahmni as the electronic medical record for the multi-country **endTB** drug-resistant tuberculosis trial, and MSF has published its own field evaluations of specific Bahmni deployments, including at Kabinda VIH Hospital in the Democratic Republic of Congo.

These are genuine hospital and multi-country humanitarian deployments. They are not evidence of Bahmni operating at the patient volume or acuity of a Canadian regional or tertiary hospital — that comparison is not established by these sources and shouldn't be implied.

## Sources

1. Thoughtworks, [“Possible”](https://www.thoughtworks.com/en-in/clients/possible), client case study describing the Bayalpata Hospital Bahmni deployment.
