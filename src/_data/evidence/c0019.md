Both the openEHR and FHIR implementer communities describe the two standards as complementary rather than competing: openEHR is positioned for deep, longitudinal clinical-data persistence and modeling, while FHIR is positioned as the exchange and interoperability layer between systems. This framing is not unique to one vendor's marketing — it appears consistently across independent bridging-tool projects built by different organizations to connect the two.

## Sources

1. openFHIR, [project site](https://open-fhir.com/). An independent open-source tool for mapping between openEHR archetypes and FHIR resources, describing the complementary relationship as its design rationale.
2. Better, [“Introducing FHIR Connect”](https://blog.better.care/introducing-fhir-connect), Better blog. A commercial vendor's articulation of the same complementary-layers framing.
